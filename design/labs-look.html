Constellation Labs

Products we built because we needed them.

Same family as Constellation. Same star, same colours, same type. Turned inside out: white paper instead of night sky.

The mark

Primary, on white
Mono, one colour
Reversed, on CI dark
Small sizes and favicon

The idea: the Constellation capsule becomes a glass test tube. The star floats inside it, the orange is the experiment, and the satin ring passes behind and in front of the glass on the diagonal. In the build, the ring turns slowly in Three.js and the liquid catches the light as you move. This file is a flat vector stand-in for that.
