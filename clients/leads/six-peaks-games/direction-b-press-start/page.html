Crew

George · Western Cape

Based in George in the Western Cape, we’re a proudly South African QA Team with a global perspective.

Our team blends lifelong gamers, competitive players, and seasoned testers across every genre and platform.

With a broad mix of backgrounds, playstyles, and perspectives, we bring well-rounded insight to every project.

Six Peaks Games is led by an experienced team with roots in global AAA studios including Eidos Interactive, Disney Interactive and Black Rock Studios, and a proven track record of creating successful, high-quality game franchises.

Each project is supported by a dedicated QA Lead, overseen by our in-studio QA Manager to maintain quality, communication, and alignment across every engagement.
