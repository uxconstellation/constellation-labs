## Our Services (8)
1. Functional QA: Testing all functional aspects of your game, including core gameplay systems, UI, progression, saves, integrations, and edge cases, to ensure stable, polished, and release-ready builds throughout development.
2. Compliance QA: Validating platform-holder requirements, including TRCs, TCRs, and Lotchecks standards, across console and storefront submissions, reducing certification risk and preventing costly resubmissions.
3. Functional Localisation QA: Testing the functional implementation of localised content, ensuring text displays correctly, fits within UI constraints, renders properly, and follows regional formatting standards without technical defects.
4. Bug Reporting & Environment Management: Providing clear, reproducible bugs with strong evidence and prioritisation, while keeping test environments, builds, accounts, and permissions organised for smooth delivery.
5. Test Case Management: Designing and maintaining structured test suites to ensure consistent coverage across features, platforms, and releases, helping teams track progress and reduce regression risk.
6. Usability Testing: Evaluating gameplay flow, readability, onboarding, accessibility considerations, and UI clarity to identify friction points and improve the overall player experience.
7. Performance Testing: Identifying crashes, frame rate instability, memory and load issues, and other stability risks, validating smooth performance across consoles, mobile devices, and representative PC configurations.
8. Release Candidate Oversight: Running final pass validation, regression checks, and sign-off support on your RC builds to confirm launch readiness and minimise day-one issues.
