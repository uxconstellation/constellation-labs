# Project ScopeScope\|

Suzerain and its Kingdom of Rizia expansion are political narrative experiences built around high-stakes choices, strategic rule and consequence-driven storytelling.

Six Peaks Games worked closely with Torpor Games to provide functional QA support across the base game and expansion, covering narrative paths, core systems and platform-specific functionality through focused test planning, exploratory testing and player issue investigation, with particular attention to console coverage, gamepad accessibility and stability across all tested platforms.
