# Project ScopeScope\|

Lost Skies is a co-op survival adventure set across floating islands, where players explore ruins, uncover lost technology, craft skyships and face the dangers of a shattered world above the clouds.

Six Peaks Games partnered with Bossa Games and The Powell Group early in development, providing rigorous QA to refine quality, strengthen core mechanics and improve the player experience.
