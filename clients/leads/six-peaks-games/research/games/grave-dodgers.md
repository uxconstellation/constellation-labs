# Project ScopeScope\|

Grave Dodgers is a chaotic 2D pixel-art dodgeball brawler for 2–4 players, where friends throw, dodge and parry their way through a silly version of hell.

Following Six Peaks Games’ work on Wild Blue through publisher Silver Lining Interactive, developer Chuhai Labs chose to partner with the team directly on its self-published title Grave Dodgers. The engagement focused on multiplayer stability, soak testing, performance coverage and player-focused feedback ahead of release.
