# Project ScopeScope\|

Godbreakers is a fast-paced co-op action roguelite where players absorb enemy powers, chain abilities and fight through chaotic runs across shifting worlds, with a strong focus on responsiveness, balance and replayability.

Six Peaks Games partnered with Thunderful to deliver broad functional, network and selective compliance testing across the full title, focusing on multiplayer as well as Epic and Steam crossplay, while also providing feedback on balance, art, first-time user experience, and new weapons and systems.
