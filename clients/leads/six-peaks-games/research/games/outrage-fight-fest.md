# Project ScopeScope\|

OutRage: Fight Fest is a fast-paced multiplayer party brawler built around chaotic physics, outrageous weapons and large-scale online combat for up to 16 players.

Hardball Games enlisted Six Peaks’ expertise to build and lead a team that functioned as an integrated QA department embedded directly into the development process of OutRage: Fight Fest. Beyond day-to-day testing, the team actively influenced gameplay refinements, helping to fine-tune character balance, hitbox accuracy, and combat pacing, and took on full QA leadership following the departure of Hardball’s QA Manager.
