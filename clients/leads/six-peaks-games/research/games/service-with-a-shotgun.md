# Project ScopeScope\|

Service with a Shotgun is a fast-paced action game where players battle fires, enemies and environmental hazards across a chaotic solo adventure. Its varied gameplay demands strong stability, progression flow and performance across PC and Steam Deck.

Six Peaks Games provided focused functional QA support across the project, helping prepare the demo for launch, support Steam Deck Verified goals and strengthen the game ahead of Gold Master release. Scope included bug reporting and verification, chapter-based coverage, full completion playthroughs, Steam Achievement validation and broader gameplay feedback.
