# Project ScopeScope\|

Town of Zoz is a narrative-driven action RPG blending combat, cooking and light farming in a vibrant fantasy world. Players follow Ito, a shaman chef returning home to help restore his town.

Following successful work on Wild Blue Skies and Lost Skies, The Powell Group again partnered with Six Peaks Games for QA support on Town of Zoz, with Six Peaks working closely with the studio as well as Balor Games to provide embedded support and actionable feedback throughout development.
