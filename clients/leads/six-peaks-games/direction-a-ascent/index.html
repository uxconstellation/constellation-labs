    The route

    From first build to launch day.

    Our embedded approach lets us learn your game inside out, building trust through smarter testing, faster iteration, and in-house consistency with the efficiency of an external partner.

    1. Base camp · Pre-production

      Test Case Management

      Designing and maintaining structured test suites to ensure consistent coverage across features, platforms, and releases, helping teams track progress and reduce regression risk.

      Climbed with

      • Yooka-Replayleewith Playtonic FriendsMulti-Level Test Coverage
      • Lost Skieswith The Powell GroupContent Coverage & Regression
      • Whimel Academywith Impossible HomeEnding & Asset Combination Coverage

    2. Camp I · Alpha

      Functional QA

      Testing all functional aspects of your game, including core gameplay systems, UI, progression, saves, integrations, and edge cases, to ensure stable, polished, and release-ready builds throughout development.

      Bug Reporting & Environment Management

      Providing clear, reproducible bugs with strong evidence and prioritisation, while keeping test environments, builds, accounts, and permissions organised for smooth delivery.

      Climbed with

      • Wild Blue Skieswith The Powell GroupCore Gameplay Testing
      • Town of Zozwith The Powell Group & Balor GamesGameplay Systems & Progression
      • It’s Only Moneywith FirestokeOpen-World Systems & Gameplay Testing

    3. Camp II · Beta

      Performance Testing

      Identifying crashes, frame rate instability, memory and load issues, and other stability risks, validating smooth performance across consoles, mobile devices, and representative PC configurations.

      Usability Testing

      Evaluating gameplay flow, readability, onboarding, accessibility considerations, and UI clarity to identify friction points and improve the overall player experience.

      Climbed with

      • Grave Dodgerswith Chuhai LabsSoak Testing
      • OutRage: Fight Festwith HardballMultiplayer Sync & Stability
      • Godbreakerswith ThunderfulMultiplayer Network & Crossplay

    4. Camp III · Localisation

      Functional Localisation QA

      Testing the functional implementation of localised content, ensuring text displays correctly, fits within UI constraints, renders properly, and follows regional formatting standards without technical defects.

      Climbed with

      • Suzerainwith Torpor GamesText Display & UI Localization Testing
      • Wild Blue Skieswith The Powell GroupGameplay-Critical Localization Testing
      • It’s Only Moneywith FirestokeLayout, Formatting & Language Presentation Testing

    5. Camp IV · Certification

      Compliance QA

      Validating platform-holder requirements, including TRCs, TCRs, and Lotchecks standards, across console and storefront submissions, reducing certification risk and preventing costly resubmissions.

      Climbed with

      • Wild Blue Skieswith The Powell GroupConsole Compliance & Certification
      • Town of Zozwith The Powell Group & Balor GamesPlatform Compliance & Certification
      • Cattle Countrywith Playtonic FriendsPlatform Compliance & Stability

    6. Summit · Launch

      Release Candidate Oversight

      Running final pass validation, regression checks, and sign-off support on your RC builds to confirm launch readiness and minimise day-one issues.

      Climbed with

      • Service with a Shotgunwith Silver Lining InteractiveFull-Game Progression & Gold Master Support
      • Godbreakerswith ThunderfulFinal Build & Submission Validation
      • Lost Skieswith The Powell GroupRelease Readiness & Risk Oversight

    7. The next peak · Post-launch and DLC

      DLC, editions and live issues

      DLC, editions and community bug investigation.

      Climbed with

      • Lil Gator Game: In the Darkwith Playtonic FriendsDLC Mechanics & Console Certification
      • Whimel Academywith Impossible HomeCommunity Bug Investigation
      • Suzerainwith Torpor GamesExploratory Testing & Player Issue Investigation

    Why us

    Why teams rope up with us

    Six Peaks Games provides rigorous, expert QA to publishers and developers worldwide. We go beyond standard QA by bringing care, consistency, and a true partnership mindset to every project, no matter the size.

    1. Technically excellent.

      A team of well-educated gamers from an exceptional local talent pool are strengthened by AAA leadership and ongoing training.

    2. Competitively priced.

      South African value aligned to Western expectations means you can stretch your QA budget further without sacrificing quality.

    3. Native English speakers.

      Clear, nuanced communication speeds up alignment and smoothes delivery while ensuring a good understanding of tone, behaviour, and intent.

    4. On Western Europe’s clock.

      Based in South Africa, we support clients worldwide, with natural time zone overlap for easier daily collaboration with game teams in Western Europe.

      GeorgeUTC+2LondonUK

    5. Culturally aligned.

      Technicians that are lifelong gamers, heavily shaped by Western entertainment, bring shared context and strong player awareness to their testing.

    6. The same team, start to finish.

      The same technicians stay on your project from start to finish with no mid-project swap-outs ensuring deep, retained knowledge and consistent communication.
